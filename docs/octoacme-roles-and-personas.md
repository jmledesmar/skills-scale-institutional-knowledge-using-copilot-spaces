# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads oversee quality assurance strategy, coordinate manual and automated testing efforts, and ensure quality gates are met before releases. They bridge the gap between development, product, and deployment teams to maintain high quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test strategy and quality standards
- Coordinate manual and automated QA efforts across the team
- Facilitate defect triage and prioritization
- Ensure Definition of Done includes appropriate quality gates
- Validate acceptance criteria are testable and met
- Report on test coverage and quality metrics
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce critical bugs in production
- Enable fast, confident deployments through automated testing
- Maintain clear quality standards and traceability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and test status updates
- Defect reports and quality metrics in sprint reviews
- Test plan reviews with Developers and Product Managers
- Release readiness assessments and deployment coordination

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Clarify acceptance criteria, report defects, collaborate on test automation
- **Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria, discuss quality trade-offs
- **Project Managers**: Provide quality status, escalate blocking issues
- **Release Manager**: Confirm QA sign-off before deployment

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers create intuitive, user-centered experiences through research, design, and validation. They ensure that features are not just functional, but also usable and delightful for end users.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability studies
- Design user flows, wireframes, and prototypes
- Lead design reviews and feedback sessions
- Collaborate on feature specifications and acceptance criteria
- Test designs with real users and iterate based on feedback
- Ensure design consistency and accessibility standards
- Document design decisions and rationale

### Goals
- Create intuitive, user-centered experiences
- Reduce user confusion and support burden
- Drive adoption and user satisfaction
- Maintain design consistency across products

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions with stakeholders and developers
- User research findings and recommendations
- Design specifications and component handoff documents
- Feedback loops during development and post-launch

### Key Interactions
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on requirements and feature prioritization
- **Developers**: Provide design specs and iterate on feasibility
- **Customer Success**: Gather user feedback and adoption insights
- **QA Lead**: Define usability acceptance criteria

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers plan, coordinate, and execute releases to production. They own deployment scheduling, incident response for failed releases, and communication throughout the release lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule release windows in coordination with stakeholders
- Own deployment coordination and runbook execution
- Manage rollback procedures and incident response for failed releases
- Communicate release schedules, status, and blockers
- Ensure pre-release checklists and smoke tests are completed
- Track release metrics and post-deployment verifications
- Document release notes and known issues

### Goals
- Execute reliable, predictable releases
- Minimize unplanned downtime and critical issues
- Enable quick incident response and recovery
- Maintain clear communication with all stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Release schedule and window announcements
- Pre-release readiness updates and blocker reports
- Deployment status and incident notifications
- Post-release retrospectives and metrics

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Confirm deployment readiness, coordinate hotfixes
- **QA Lead**: Verify quality sign-off before deployment
- **Project Manager**: Align on timelines and escalate blockers
- **Customer Success**: Communicate release impact and feature availability
- **Support**: Coordinate incident response and customer notifications

---

## Customer Success

### Role Summary
Customer Success teams bridge the gap between product development and end users. They gather user feedback, monitor adoption, and provide actionable insights that drive continuous improvement and customer satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and synthesize customer feedback and usage patterns
- Monitor product adoption and identify training or support gaps
- Communicate customer needs and pain points to product teams
- Support customer onboarding and feature training
- Track customer satisfaction and identify at-risk accounts
- Provide data-driven insights for product prioritization
- Collaborate on post-launch validation and user enablement

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and retention
- Drive product adoption and usage
- Reduce support burden through proactive engagement
- Enable data-driven product decisions through customer insights

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and trend reports
- Feature adoption and usage metrics
- Customer success and satisfaction scores
- Regular syncs with Product and Project Management teams
- Release communication and user enablement materials

### Key Interactions
- **Product Managers**: Share customer insights and prioritization input
- **Project Manager**: Align on release timing and adoption strategy
- **UX Designer**: Provide user feedback and usability insights
- **Release Manager**: Communicate release impact and feature availability
- **Support Teams**: Coordinate on customer issues and training needs
